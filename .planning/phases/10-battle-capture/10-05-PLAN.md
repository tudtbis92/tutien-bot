Purpose: every player-facing random outcome in this phase lives here under the crypto mandate; every fee goes through the wallet; every attempt is audited. This is the phase's security-critical core (ASVS V6 cryptography + repudiation).

Output: battleCheckInService + captureService + boss stat constants + unit tests (mock-tx pattern).
</objective>
