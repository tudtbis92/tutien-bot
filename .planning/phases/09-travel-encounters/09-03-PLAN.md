<objective>
What this plan accomplishes: the **pull-based travel check-in engine (TQC-07)** — `checkInTravel(userId)` computes an active journey's elapsed time, rolls 1× per counted minute (delegating to `encounterService` from 09-04), decrements the pause-aware remaining-seconds clock (D-07/D-25), stops at the first encounter hit (D-24), and resolves arrival inline (D-05/D-28). The full result dispatch (status/encounter/arrival) lands in `/sanguo travel`; the arrival embed is returned inline (D-23). NO cron, NO notification service.

Purpose: close the loop started in 09-01 — a journey the player starts must resolve (or surface encounters) when the player checks in, entirely on their shard. This is the arrival-resolution + pause half of the pull model.

Output: check-in engine + arrival embed + i18n arrival keys + ack/resume handling + unit tests.
</objective>
