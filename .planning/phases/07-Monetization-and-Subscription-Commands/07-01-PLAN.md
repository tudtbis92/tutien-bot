## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-07-01 | Tampering | User Balance | mitigate | Use atomic SQL `UPDATE ... WHERE balance >= fee` with `.returning()` check — empty result means insufficient balance. Prevents double-spending or negative balance. |
| T-07-02 | Denial of Service | Subscription Checks | mitigate | Ensure expiry checks in `SelfBotMaster` are efficient and don't block the polling loop. |
| T-07-05 | Tampering | Upgrade Fee | mitigate | Guard `calculateUpgradeFee` — reject upgrade when `daysLeft <= 0` to prevent free upgrade on expired plans. |
